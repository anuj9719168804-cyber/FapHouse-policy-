✨ 1. Overview

This policy describes possible information handling when you interact with FapHouse Downloader Bot on Telegram. Actual processing depends on the bot's deployed code, configuration, and connected services. The operator should keep this page accurate as the service changes.

📥 2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the implementation, the bot may process:

  • Telegram user ID and messages sent to the bot;
  • links submitted for processing and related media metadata;
  • temporary files created to deliver a requested result; and
  • technical logs, such as timestamps and error reports.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unrelated sensitive information to the bot.

⚙️ 3. How information may be used

Information may be used to process submitted links, return results, troubleshoot failures, maintain reliability, prevent misuse, and respond to support requests.

🔗 4. Third-party services

Processing a link may involve Telegram, the linked website, hosting providers, or other services used by the bot. These services may receive the technical information needed to handle the request and are governed by their own privacy terms.

🗄️ 5. Storage, security, and retention

Information and temporary files may be processed or stored for periods determined by the deployed configuration. The operator should document actual retention periods, access controls, and deletion practices. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

🤝 6. Sharing of information

Information may be transmitted to service providers when needed to operate the bot, or disclosed where required by applicable law. The operator must ensure this description matches the bot's actual practices.

🧑‍💻 7. Your choices and data requests

You may stop using the bot at any time. For questions about information associated with your use, or to request deletion where applicable, contact the developer using the support details below. Some information may remain in backups or technical logs for a limited period.

🛡️ 8. Appropriate use and age restrictions

Use the bot only where you are legally permitted to access the linked service and content. Follow applicable laws, age restrictions, and the linked service's terms. Do not use the bot to access content unlawfully or without authorization.

🔄 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the bot's features or data practices change. The date at the top should be updated whenever a material revision is published.
